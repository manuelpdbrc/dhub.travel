Strategic Clarity · FAQ 04 / 05

Clear answers for agile decisions.

How we integrate with hotels, operators, investors, and destinations in Patagonia to accelerate positioning and return.

What types of companies, projects, and destinations do we partner with in Patagonia? +
We partner with five core profiles across Patagonia and South America: hotels, lodges, estancias, and glampings; inbound tour operators (DMCs) and experience providers; investors and developers of new tourism projects; conservation initiatives and parks; and destinations, municipalities, or tourism boards seeking to professionalize their stewardship.
Can we hire a specific bespoke service or a full turnkey project? +
Both options are available. We can lead an end-to-end turnkey development—from market research, business plan, and feasibility to commercial launch and staff training—or join through bespoke modules where your team needs immediate executive capacity.
What sets the DSO (Destination Stewardship) approach apart from traditional consulting? +
Conventional consulting often delivers lengthy reports without execution while promoting mass volume. At Destination Hub, we co-execute alongside your team prioritizing Yield (net economic value per visitor over volume), data-backed decisions, and a regenerative proposition anchored in agroecology, living culture, nature, and the local community.
How does your B2B Commercial Representation and Marketing service work? +
We act as your external commercial arm. We connect your hotel, lodge, experience, or destination with wholesale tour operators, DMCs, travel consortia, and key source markets, aligning B2B commercial agreements with digital campaigns focused on direct conversion and deseasonalization.
